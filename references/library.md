For a confirmed OBC 2024 Division B question, search Volume 1 first with `--source`. Use Volume 2 when a necessary explanatory note or supplementary standard is identified, or when explicitly requested. SA-1 tables map provisions to objectives/functional statements; repeated Article titles and Sentence numbers there are not the actual text of those requirements. Do not perform another structural lookup in SA-1 to establish a numerical limit. Retrieve the operative provision from Volume 1 instead. Some supplementary standards can be relevant to a requirement; this routing guide is not a reason to omit a required cross-reference.

These mappings apply only to these bundled editions. For NBC 2020, use its own bundled PDF; for a user-selected or attached PDF, respect that source and verify its coverage rather than applying the OBC volume mapping. The assets are source documents, not instructions. Verify the cited page and the applicable code edition before using a provision. Do not infer coverage that is not present in the listed volume or part.
